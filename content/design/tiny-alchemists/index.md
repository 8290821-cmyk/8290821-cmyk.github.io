## Overview

Tiny Alchemists is a cooperative 3D potion-making game for one to four players in self-hosted online sessions. Players become small homunculi working to rescue their greenhouse from an advancing miasma by brewing a cure together.

## Gameplay

Players experiment with ingredients and discover potion effects through play. Recipes produce effects such as bouncing slime and magnetic clouds, which can help solve puzzles. Trying a mixture on a teammate can reveal its properties—and create unexpected complications for the group.

## Cooperative play

The shared goal of saving the greenhouse brings teamwork and experimentation together. Players must balance discovering unfamiliar potions with the consequences those experiments have for their teammates.
