Professional Summary

I am a master’s student in Interactive Media Technology at KTH Royal Institute of Technology, with a background in product design. My research interests lie in immersive and embodied interaction, human–AI interaction, and social interaction in games. I explore how people perceive, respond to, and make decisions when interacting with virtual agents and AI systems. My recent work examines social agents in VR, visible self-repair in conversational AI, and prosocial lying in games. I combine interactive prototyping with user studies and behavioural data analysis to understand these interactions and inform their design.
